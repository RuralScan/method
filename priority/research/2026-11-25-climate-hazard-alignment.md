## What the project sources establish

The jointly authored Stocktake Review, sections 4.7.1–4.7.2, distinguishes physical hazards from exposure and vulnerability. It names temperature extremes, precipitation variability, flooding, drought and coastal processes; it describes both magnitude and frequency, with flood depth, drought and wind speed as examples. It does not specify exact indices, averaging periods or class cutoffs. This supports the conceptual distinction below, not attribution of a specific metric or dataset to Brayden.
