# Other erosion datasets: scope and fitness for RuralScan

26 November 2026. Pete requested comparison while authorising extraction and checks of the GloSEM 1.3 cropland raster. This is a bounded source comparison, not a change to the nine core variables or approved four soil-loss bands. Infrastructure remains in scope, with its existing national-data acquisition needs and provisional sources.

## Useful alternatives

| Dataset | Coverage / support / period | What it measures | Fit and limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GloSEM global v1.1** | Broad model support including cropland, forest and natural areas; 2001/2012; public files resampled to approximately **25 km** | Modelled water-erosion soil loss, tonnes/ha/year | Closest broad-land-system family to the approved erosion interpretation. Public coarse raster cannot provide original 250 m model detail. Existing candidate still needs acquisition and native checks. [Producer catalogue](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/global-soil-erosion) |
| **GloSEM 2015 baseline and 2070 projections** | Broad global model; producer advertises approximately **25 km** distributed support; 2015 baseline with separate future scenarios | Modelled water-erosion rates under baseline or land-use/climate scenarios | A newer broad-coverage baseline to assess alongside 2012. Select baseline explicitly; never mix a future scenario into present characterisation. Native file details and use conditions still need checking. [Producer catalogue](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/global-soil-erosion-water-2070) |
| **Global multi-year RUSLE soil loss (2026 release)** | Global, **2.5 arc-minutes (~4 km)**; historical averages 1960–1990 and 1990–2020, plus separate 2080–2100 scenarios | Modelled multi-year water-erosion soil-loss rates | Potential intermediate-resolution broad-coverage candidate. Public v2 record specifies CC BY 4.0 and supplies MATLAB files; native masks, units/encoding and spatial orientation are not inspected. Record still describes its direct supporting paper as in minor revision, so do not imply completed peer review. Screening only, not a selected replacement. [Author repository record](https://zenodo.org/records/23355150) |
| **EPM / modified EPM** | Global; **0.0083 degrees (roughly 1 km)**; reference 2010, published 2024 | Gross/net erosion modelling involving additional processes, including gullies and soil slumps | Useful alternative model for wider erosion context. Not interchangeable with RUSLE sheet/rill erosion: do not transfer the current class interpretation automatically. Producer flags cold-region limitations for EPM and substantial catchment-level errors even in mEPM. [Producer catalogue](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/erosion-potential-method-epm) |
| **Multiple co-occurring erosion processes** | Global cropland/farmable-land scenarios; **1 km**; source temporal coverage 2010–2020, released 2023 | Five susceptibility classes for water, wind, gully, tillage and crop-harvesting erosion, plus co-occurrence | Useful supplementary diagnosis of which process matters. These are susceptibility classes, not tonnes/ha/year; no direct 1/3/5 crosswalk. Intrinsic farmable-land and actual-cropland scenarios must remain distinct. [Producer catalogue](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/multiple-co-occurring-erosion-processes-global-cropland) |
| **RUSLE2015 / European water erosion** | EU28; **100 m**; 2010 and 2016 | Modelled water-erosion soil loss with European inputs | Potential regional override for European applications; provides no Haiti coverage. [Producer catalogue](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/soil-erosion-water-rusle2015) |
| **GLASOD** | Global expert-assessed polygons; approximately **1:10 million** map scale; 1990 assessment | Human-induced soil degradation type, extent and degree, including water/wind erosion | Historical background only for this task: old, coarse and not a current gridded soil-loss rate. [ISRIC](https://isric.org/projects/global-assessment-human-induced-soil-degradation-glasod) |
| **Haiti national/local mapping** | CNIGS/HaitiData local gully and bank-erosion lines, **March 2021**; catalogue names Cayes-Jacmel/La Vallée, but returned commune labels cover Cayes-Jacmel only | Indicative local hazard classes attached to mapped linear features | Valuable local context and a route to national custodians; not nationwide erosion rates or a substitute across all Haiti. Metadata licence is unspecified, so deployment/redistribution remain unresolved. [HaitiData dataset 28](https://opendata.haitidata.org/catalogue/#/dataset/28) |

The currently inspected **GloSEM 1.3 2019 cropland product (~100 m)** remains the finer-rate candidate for its supported cropland footprint. Its public author release is CC0. The older/broader GloSEM products merit assessment for forest/natural-area context; mEPM is an alternative erosion concept, not a resolution-only replacement. National sources should replace or refine these where suitable, after checking geography, dates, units, process definition and use conditions.

Rainfall erosivity (R-factor), soil erodibility (K-factor), terrain factors and SoilGrids soil properties are **inputs or drivers**, not ready-made soil-loss rates. Do not bind those fields to the existing tonnes/ha/year classes. Sediment yield delivered to a river also differs from soil detached on a hillslope; infrastructure exposure does not establish sediment damage or protection benefit.

## Haiti follow-up actually checked

The previously acquired full CNIGS/HaitiData catalogue was screened for erosion/ravinement. It includes the national hazard atlas and three Cayes-Jacmel indicative map documents, plus dataset 28, `geonode:Ravinement`. A public WFS request returned **4,034 records**, matching `totalFeatures`, in EPSG:4326: 4,033 valid MultiLineString geometries and one missing geometry. Although the catalogue names Cayes-Jacmel and La Vallée, all returned `Commune` attributes say Cayes-Jacmel; do not claim that this extract establishes coverage of both. Native labels are Danger faible (3,052), Danger moyen (631), Pas de danger (215) and Danger eleve (136); these describe the mapped linear hazard, not tonnes/ha/year. Fields include `fid`, `id`, `ravinement`, `Section`, `Commune`, `Auteur`, `Remarque`. This establishes an available local layer, not a complete national inventory or universal class translation. Observation metadata names March 2021; the catalogue's November 2026 publication timestamp must not replace that observation date.

A CNIGS national erosion-risk map is referenced in earlier Haiti project documentation, but its current authoritative GIS package and class definitions were not acquired in this bounded search. The four approved RuralScan water-erosion classes cannot be assigned to named local hazard categories without a reviewed crosswalk.

## Hosted-route screening

A published agroforestry study links an author Earth Engine asset, `users/saraheb3/AFSuitabilityTool/GloSEM_clipped_output`. Its live metadata was checked: it is a geographically clipped, reprojected EPSG:3857 image for the continental-US study context, not a verified global source covering Haiti. It therefore does not replace the original Figshare raster for this task. Its separate unit-scaled asset is also unsuitable as the raw tonnes/ha/year input. No study-specific weights or US calibration were adopted. Source: [author repository](https://github.com/saraheb3/AgroforestrySuitability_GEE), `gee_script/Agroforestry-Suitability-Map`, original asset declaration and scaling sections; live metadata preserved in the audit.
