We are the Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT, CGIAR, developing the World Bank-funded Rural Nature-based Solutions Scan: a research methodology and demonstrator to help public-sector teams scope investment opportunities in rural, agricultural and forestry landscapes. We request current KBA polygons for Haiti and all sites intersecting a 5 km buffer beyond its boundary, including neighbouring-country sites, with release date, stable site identifiers, coverage and geometry documentation.

We will inspect coverage, geometry and overlap, then calculate proximity classes: within KBA including boundary; outside but less than 1.5 km; 1.5–5 km inclusive; and beyond 5 km where search coverage is valid. One biodiversity screening layer will retain the higher of KBA proximity and Biodiversity Intactness Index depletion classes. Missing data remain unknown; KBA proximity is not a legal exclusion or proof of intervention suitability.

Platforms and outputs: reproducible Jupyter/Colab notebooks, methodology toolkit, Haiti pilot report, static maps and summary statistics, plus a demonstrator potentially displaying derived proximity/combined layers.
