# Third-party material in the carbon/Naturebase research records

The repository's MIT licence covers original project code and documentation. It does not relicense third-party datasets, retained source code or sampled source-derived values. Attribution, source/version locators and acquisition hashes are preserved in each report and audit; no global rasters or ZIPs are included here.

- **Naturebase:** catalogue conditions are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Retained Naturebase raster samples and Haiti administrative extracts remain under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), with the pathway-specific source attribution in each `catalogue_record(s).json` and linked report. Local JSON extraction, sampling and diagnostic summaries are modifications for this research record, not producer-certified releases. Source-specific upstream conditions also remain relevant. These records do not establish compatibility with every later project use. [Naturebase](https://www.naturebase.org/about).
- **Soils Revealed soil carbon futures v3:** Sanderman and colleagues (2020), [Harvard Dataverse dataset](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/HA17D3), CC BY 4.0. Retained samples and native Haiti subsets derive from its no-till, higher-input and joint files. Source filenames, producer MD5 and local SHA256 are recorded; sampling/summary calculations are our modifications. Licence acceptance is documented in component provenance.
- **Biochar from crop residues v1:** [Harvard Dataverse source](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/Y6NGFM), CC0 1.0. Retained README, sample values and summaries retain the source attribution and units.
- **Soils Revealed application code:** `soc-stock-future-change.js`, `soc-stock-future-period.js` and the renamed snapshot `soils_revealed_constants.js` are retained from Vizzuality/soils-revealed, commit `9eb9e12a434d997edaacd52f935d0d5fe9a819d7`. Copyright (c) 2020 Vizzuality, MIT. The [complete upstream notice](crop-soil-components-audit-20261126/soils_revealed_LICENSE.txt) is included. Source code was inspected, not used to redefine the dataset's licence.
- **Shared diagnostic inputs:** the [input README](haiti-diagnostic-inputs-20261126/README.md) records public-domain LSIB boundary terms and ESA WorldCover CC BY 4.0 attribution. Points are generated diagnostic samples, not private observations.

Other source metadata, published catalogue fields and small diagnostic observations retain the source URLs and conditions recorded in their reports. No new licence grant is asserted for those sources. Reacquire large originals through their documented official routes and retain source conditions during any subsequent binding release.
