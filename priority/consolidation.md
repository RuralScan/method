## Current routing

Pete's explicit directions are recorded in `routing-directions.json`, without creating a human decision receipt or threshold acceptance:

- IPLC land overlap is an **unscored descriptor**, with mapped presence, independently verified absence, or unknown. Opportunity-space characterization may display it; Project risk carries safeguard follow-up and Next steps carries consultation/consent requirements. Presence is neither a preference score nor an automatic exclusion. Existing reference chapters already route overlap through M3, M2b and M6. Their automatic FPIC wording requires policy-specific interpretation downstream; this change does not purport to establish legal applicability.
- Carbon **loss/degradation need** must be distinguished from stock and counterfactual mitigation potential. The legacy potential ID stays visible for traceability, with its scoring blocked until a separate successor is defined. No stock raster is relabeled as loss. Practice-specific Naturebase potential may inform descriptive opportunity-space characterization or Next steps after binding verification.
- Drought and water classifications are supported by the research dossier, not arbitrary requested cutoffs. Crop/pasture-season stress, affected area and recurrence are distinct from mean standardized meteorological anomalies.

The current table has 19 IDs: 14 priority candidates (including the held carbon successor slot) and five descriptors. The historical source CSV remains unchanged. This sidecar is not a runtime configuration update.

## Release protection

A future accepted proposal must pin all five definition CSVs **plus** the current T5 reference CSV and routing-directions JSON. The package validator checks current and selected hashes, blocks unscored descriptors and unresolved successor variables, and matches each rule's concern to its current row. Old proposal bytes are preserved; they cannot bypass the new dependency requirements. Source keeping, binding acceptance, exact classification/context acceptance and storage acceptance remain necessary.

No accepted rules, official source registrations, votes or runtime edits are included. New research observations still require normal acquisition and source review before they can support a release. See [research dossier](research/2026-11-25-livelihood-classification.md).
