## Why an override is proposed

An override records a deliberate relationship when pooling literature claims would not answer the intended suitability question. It must explain its scope, evidence, mechanism, quantitative representation and transfer assumptions. A source can inform the reasoning without having measured the proposed response. Source-reported relationships and the analyst's choices therefore remain separate. [src: evidence:suitability/overrides/README.md §Provenance and remaining review]

The records below are pending review inputs. They do not change accepted mappings, activate a dataset binding or alter opportunity hectares. The same pinned records appear in the dashboard's Rule overrides view. Historical analytical tables and current finding reviews keep their separate editions. [src: evidence:suitability/overrides/README.md §Dashboard and methods integration]

## Four distinct decisions

Table: Decisions between a pending rule and an active spatial calculation.

| Decision | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Rule proposal | The relationship, justification and exact scope are available for review | Scientific acceptance |
| Scientific acceptance | An eligible human accepts the exact proposal and its stated applicability | That a runtime can execute it |
| Technical implementation | A representation and reference evaluator reproduce the authored rules | Ecological validation or activation in a scan |
| Runtime activation | A supported consumer explicitly uses accepted mappings and bindings in a pinned configuration | Site-level feasibility |

Source admission and evidence Keep belong to their own records. Neither accepts an override. A successful software test or merge does not supply scientific approval. The current candidates are pending and inactive; their reference representation is implemented, while production spatial integration remains separate. ## Read scope before interpreting a score

A candidate may name several practices, with explicit family, subpractice, pathway and contextual limits. Official accepted proposals still address one current family at a time. Missing or mismatched scope cannot silently inherit an exclusion. In particular, retaining existing parkland trees does not imply the recruitment constraints proposed for regeneration to upright trees. [src: evidence:suitability/overrides/README.md §Pending rules created]

The climate candidates separate continuous membership from named exclusion screens and conditional water pathways. Their nested minimum does not permit warmth to compensate for dryness or moisture to compensate for cold. The temperature-only derivation, climate-class transfer and mixed historical input periods are declared policy choices with limitations; the memberships are not survival probabilities. Missing inputs stay unknown, and an independently known host, legal or cold exclusion remains visible. [src: evidence:suitability/overrides/README.md §Pending rules created]

The mangrove candidate preserves the archived native index and reports the source's estimated restorable hectares separately. Its typological polygons support a score; they do not delineate restoration footprints. Partial units without verified allocation retain bounds rather than a point estimate. Neither score multiplication nor polygon-area fractions establish restoration hectares. A spatial adapter and supported eligible-loss footprint are required before pixel-level opportunity intersection. [src: evidence:suitability/overrides/README.md §Mangrove area and release safeguards]

## Inspect the decision trail

For each candidate, inspect the source edition and locator, what it reports, the mechanism interpretation, authored choices, transfer limits and binding assumptions. The references are not fabricated evidence units. Any missing admission, calibration or transfer decision stays explicit. Exact prior mapping fingerprints, dependencies and supersession must be resolved through the official proposal and acceptance route before replacement; a candidate's intention to replace a variable is not a withdrawal.
