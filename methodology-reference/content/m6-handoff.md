## Question this module answers

Module 6 (M6) turns the shortlist into questions for local assessment: what should be checked, with whom, and before which decision? The scan identifies promising options. It does not establish feasibility or require every shortlisted practice to proceed. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §1] [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §2]

## Inputs

Use the selected practices and ranked areas, evidence-supported benefits and costs, investment-risk flags, data qualifications and retained findings for unmapped practices. Keep the evidence and application versions with the brief. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §3] [src: method:handbook/implement-a-scan.md §3. Build the scoping brief]

## Method

### 1. State the stage pathway

Place the result in its decision sequence: scoping, pre-feasibility, feasibility and design, then implementation. State what the scan supports and which assessments are still needed before investment. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §6]

### 2. Give the indicative economic snapshot

Carry forward only the cost measures supported by [[table:T6E]], with currency, period and denominator. Preserve insufficient-evidence states. Do not calculate new cost-effectiveness ratios from unrelated benefit and cost rows; cost per tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent avoided is outside this scoping output. [[ch:cost-profiles]] gives the reporting rules. [src: methodology/modules/M5_scorecard.md §10] [src: schema/spec.md §T6]

### 3. List recommended next steps, each tied to a scan output

For each shortlisted area, link the finding to the next assessment. A priority rank calls for field checks; a tenure flag calls for stakeholder and rights assessment; an uncertain cost calls for local design and costing. Include land availability, safeguards and financing rather than treating the map as an investment decision. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §6] [src: method:handbook/implement-a-scan.md §3. Build the scoping brief]

### 4. Signpost feasibility methods and tools

Choose assessment depth to match the decision. Early screening can use documented proxies; shortlisted sites may need detailed carbon, erosion, ecosystem-service or economic modelling. Tools named in the source guidance are options, not endorsements or substitutes for local expertise. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §6]

### 5. Add the design step for tree-based practices

Identify the species or mixture, planting or regeneration approach, establishment needs and resilience to future climate. A family-level suitability map does not choose the right tree for a field. Retained species evidence can support later design; crop evidence enters practice suitability only where its policy allows it. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §10] [src: AGENTS.md §What is locked]

### 6. Tailor to the context

Carry relevant operational barriers from [[ch:m2b-project-risk]] into consultations and pre-feasibility checks. Explain what an investment in access, extension or finance would need to achieve. These questions do not become structural suitability exclusions. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §10] [src: AGENTS.md §What is locked]

### 7. State the scope boundary

End the brief with the decision it supports and what remains unverified. Scoping flags where further work is worthwhile; feasibility validates the local investment case. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §6]

## The sensitive-variable recommendation

Identify population, poverty, production and other inputs that need confirmation with country-endorsed sources. Keep this question even when a metadata flag is missing. [[ch:farming-systems-and-data]] explains data sensitivity; the scan does not validate national statistics. [src: methodology/T4_generation_method.md §2.6]

## The safeguard trigger

Mapped overlap with Indigenous Peoples' and local community land must carry the recorded `FPIC_REQUIRED` flag into the hand-off and scorecard. Explain it as a prompt for free, prior and informed consent (FPIC) and World Bank Environmental and Social Standard 7 assessment. These procedures are not completed by the scan. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §10] [src: methodology/modules/M5_scorecard.md §10]

Coverage is incomplete. Absence of a mapped polygon never clears the safeguard question or establishes that no people or rights are affected. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §10]

## Guidance for families the scan does not map

For a family marked `qualitative_only`, state why a landscape map would mislead, what retained evidence says, and what local siting or design assessment is needed. Such evidence remains available for this hand-off even where the family is excluded from landscape T3/T6 outputs. Cross-family evidence labels are not additional practices. [[table:FAM]] provides the definitions. [src: methodology/T4_generation_method.md §2.4] [src: AGENTS.md §What is locked]

For linear practices, report candidate length, density or placement zones as appropriate, not the full area of raster cells. [[ch:m1-suitability]] explains footprint measures. [src: methodology/T4_generation_method.md §2.4]

## Feasibility notes

Retained findings can reveal design needs that a suitability score does not express. The source-linked feasibility notes include lessons about complementary livelihood measures, sediment maintenance and income forgone when wetland use changes. Read each in its original context rather than generalising it to every project. [src: methodology/feasibility_notes.md]

A useful note states the practice, finding, design implication and source. Bundled interventions must not be credited wholly to one practice, and income from existing use belongs in the comparison baseline. See [[ch:effect-synthesis]] for attribution and compatibility. [src: AGENTS.md §What is locked]

## Outputs

Table: What to carry into local assessment.

| Part of the brief | Content |
|---|---|
| Shortlist | Named areas and practices, why they merit investigation, and what remains unknown. |
| Evidence and costs | Supported effects, confidence, applicability and indicative costs with units. |
| Follow-up | Field checks, tenure and consultation needs, safeguards, design and financing questions. |
| Data qualifications | Missing inputs, substitutions, sensitive statistics and valid footprint measures. |
| Unmapped options | Retained evidence and the local assessment needed to consider them. |

## Assumptions and limits

The brief inherits the scan's data gaps and transfer uncertainties. A missing record or flag is not proof that an issue is absent. Feasibility must confirm land, rights, consent, local effects and costs, then decide whether and how the practice should proceed. [src: methodology/modules/M6_handoff.md §10] [src: method:handbook/implement-a-scan.md §3. Build the scoping brief]
