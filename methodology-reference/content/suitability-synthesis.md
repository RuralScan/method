## What produces a T4 mapping

An AI agent will derive proposed suitability mappings from the evidence registry, working to a versioned synthesis skill. For each suitability family, variable and context, it must interpret the source claims, decide what can be combined, propose the simplest defensible representation and make the reasoning inspectable. Scientific reviewers approve the interpretation. Deterministic code checks and packages the accepted mapping; the runtime then evaluates it against spatial data.

These are different responsibilities. Schema validation can establish that a mapping is well formed. It cannot decide whether a source supports a global threshold or whether two response profiles are ecologically comparable. Those are synthesis judgements that the agent must explain and reviewers must assess. The existing synthesis skill and review contract provide the evidence-retention, plausibility and decision-record requirements for that work. [src: .agents/skills/source-command-t4-synthesise/SKILL.md] [src: .agents/skills/extraction_contracts/T4_review_contract.md]

The adopted workflow is agent-led synthesis, not automatic derivation through the old filtering, fixed-weight and weighted-median routines. The rule being produced is still a T4 suitability mapping: a relationship, mask or other supported representation that a runtime can use. Calling its output a rule does not mean that a fixed algorithm made the scientific synthesis decision.

## Inputs and the decision to be made

Start from the last accepted mapping and decision for the same family, variable and spatial context. Retrieve the candidate evidence, source passages and complete quantitative profiles, including contrary findings, scope restrictions and review holds. Check the family definition, canonical quantity, available dataset binding and intended spatial product. A slope measured at plot scale, for example, must not silently become the same quantity as mean slope in a coarse grid cell. [src: .agents/skills/source-command-t4-synthesise/SKILL.md §Translating evidence into a rule]

Assess three questions separately: whether a source supports using the variable, whether it supports a suitability direction, and whether it supports numerical calibration. A source can contribute useful direction or conditional numerical information without independently establishing a final global curve. Keep complete source profiles available for comparison; do not reduce every regional source to qualitative evidence merely because transfer needs further reasoning. [src: .agents/skills/extraction_contracts/T4_review_contract.md §Three independent assessments] [src: .agents/skills/extraction_contracts/T4_review_contract.md §Retain complete quantitative source recommendations]

The shared appraisal, compatibility and dependence considerations are in [[ch:evidence-principles]] and [[ch:compiling-evidence]]. This chapter applies them to the particular task of proposing a T4 mapping. It does not prescribe the T3/T6 effect or T6E economic synthesis methods.

## How the agent handles candidate evidence

The agent must account for every candidate's contribution or non-use. The following table gives questions and required dispositions for review, not an automatic filtering algorithm. Existing scope and approval constraints still apply; agent judgement is not permission to invent missing evidence or bypass a hold.

Table: Candidate evidence and the interpretation the T4 synthesis agent must document.

| Candidate situation | Required interpretation and record |
|---|---|
| Dropped evidence or an unresolved review hold | Respect the recorded status. Explain any proposed reopening through the review process; do not silently restore approval |
| Observed occurrence or a study-site descriptor | Use it as context where informative. Establish whether a separate source statement supports a generalising siting requirement; occurrence alone supplies no failure boundary |
| Species- or crop-specific requirements | Preserve taxon and scope. Apply the declared crop-admission policy; do not turn a species envelope into a general practice rule |
| Access, labour, tenure or another enabling condition | Distinguish operational risk from structural suitability. Keep investment-addressable conditions in the appropriate implementation/risk lane; distinguish hard legal exclusions |
| Model importance or a source-model weight | Retain as variable-use or importance evidence. Do not convert it into a suitability score, response shape or default runtime weight |
| Complete quantitative profile from a regional source | Preserve native classes, boundaries and conditions. Assess compatibility and transfer explicitly rather than automatically promoting or discarding the profile |
| Ambiguous units, unclear endpoints or an unsupported old encoding | Return to the source, retain reported values and state the unresolved interpretation. A decoder failure is not evidence that the source contains no useful information |
| Several claims sharing a study, dataset or judgement origin | Preserve distinct information, identify dependence and justify its influence; repeated rows or author names alone do not establish independence |

## Constructing and comparing the proposal

Group evidence by intervention, measured quantity and statistic, spatial support, relevant context and underlying origin. A common unit is necessary for some comparisons but insufficient to establish compatibility. Explain why profiles can be combined, why they should remain context-specific, or why the evidence cannot yet support a settled mapping. [src: .agents/skills/source-command-t4-synthesise/SKILL.md §Translating evidence into a rule]

Propose a representation that the evidence supports: for example, a scoped range, an ordinal profile, a categorical gate, a continuous response or a qualitative statement with a calibration gap. The agent must specify aggregation, endpoint inclusion, missing-value treatment, dataset binding and context overrides. It must distinguish a source's reported values from any transformation or interpretation it proposes. Direction-only evidence cannot supply a numerical endpoint, interpolation rule or default score. [src: .agents/skills/extraction_contracts/T4_review_contract.md §Three independent assessments]

There is no instruction here to average all thresholds or multiply them by the historical compiler's credibility constants. If the agent proposes a numerical pooling method or weights, it must justify that choice for the compatible evidence group, document source dependence, retain disagreement and show relevant sensitivity. Suitability class scores are not measured probabilities simply because they are expressed between zero and one. The historical factors remain available for comparison and reproduction, not as unexamined defaults for the agent.

Keep evidence influence, within-study importance and runtime criterion weights separate. A source assigning 30% to slope in its own model has not established a 30% Rural Scan weight. Nor has a source-model zero automatically established a hard exclusion for all landscapes. Each proposed mapping or weight needs its own meaning and approval scope. [src: methodology/T4_variable_importance.md] [src: methodology/T4_developer_contract.md]

### Worked illustration: context is not a boundary

In this hypothetical illustration, one account documents a practice between 600 and 900 mm annual rainfall, while a regional study reports several rainfall suitability classes. The agent should preserve the first as occurrence context and the second as a quantitative source profile. It must inspect the class meanings, measurement basis, host system, geography and uncertainty before proposing any synthesis. It cannot infer global failure below 600 mm, extend the regional classes into unobserved tails, or average the two ranges as equivalent thresholds.

Table: Hypothetical rainfall evidence, proposed disposition and unresolved question.

| Evidence available | What the agent can propose | What remains to resolve |
|---|---|---|
| Occurrence between 600 and 900 mm only | Retain the observed setting as context | No source-supported failure limits or suitability scores |
| Regional class profile with clear units and complete endpoints | Preserve the native profile as a candidate for its stated context | Measurement compatibility, scientific basis and transfer |
| Reviewed compatible profiles from independent origins | Propose a scoped combination or explain why alternatives should remain separate | Justification of the chosen representation and scientific approval |

The defensible outcome may be a context-specific profile, a qualitative direction with a calibration hold, or a supported proposal assembled from additional compatible evidence already in scope. Missing evidence is recorded as a gap. Synthesis does not itself authorise another extraction or discovery sweep. [src: .agents/skills/source-command-t4-synthesise/SKILL.md §Reproducible workflow]

## Review, validation and acceptance

Reviewers assess the source interpretation and the proposed mapping, not merely whether its JSON passes a validator. Numeric plausibility review examines measurement precision, class widths, mechanism, proxy use, abrupt transitions and transfer. A faithful quotation or a generic Keep decision does not approve a curve, an exclusion or its global application. [src: .agents/skills/extraction_contracts/T4_review_contract.md §Numeric plausibility gate]

Table: Responsibilities between evidence interpretation and a reproducible T4 release.

| Stage | Responsibility | What it cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Agent synthesis | Propose the mapping and document evidence use, assumptions, alternatives and uncertainty | Human approval or local validation |
| Scientific review | Accept, query or hold the interpretation and its stated scope | Successful execution on every dataset or site |
| Deterministic validation | Check schema, references, units, allowed representation, parameter consistency and recorded holds | Ecological compatibility or scientific validity of a threshold |
| Compilation and packaging | Encode accepted decisions and reproduce the declared outputs | A new synthesis decision or permission to fill missing parameters |
| Runtime evaluation | Apply the accepted mapping to its declared spatial inputs | Site-level feasibility or transfer beyond the approved context |

[src: methodology/T4_developer_contract.md] [src: .agents/skills/source-command-t4-synthesise/SKILL.md]

Check illustrative sites at each disputed boundary and on either side, including exact endpoints and missing coverage. Compare proposed outputs with the previous accepted mapping. Retain masks separately from compensatory scoring, preserve contextual restrictions, and report what changes and why. A proposed revision supersedes an accepted decision explicitly; it does not overwrite the evidence record. [src: .agents/skills/source-command-t4-synthesise/SKILL.md §Translating evidence into a rule]

## Reproducibility of an agent-led decision

Archive the evidence and policy revisions, task or prompt, skill version, actual model/settings where available, candidate dispositions, proposal, rationale, review decision and accepted mapping. Do not promise that a fresh language-model run will reproduce the same scientific judgement. Reproducibility rests on retaining the accepted decision and replaying its validated representation. New evidence, context or interpretation creates a new proposal with an explicit comparison. [src: methodology/T4_generation_method.md §Synthesis decision record] [src: .agents/skills/source-command-t4-synthesise/SKILL.md]

## Assumptions and limits

An agent can omit relevant evidence, misinterpret source classes or produce an unsupported synthesis despite correct citations. Complete candidate accounting, source review, boundary examples and scientific approval remain essential. Numerical validation cannot compensate for a weak ecological argument, and more dependent sources cannot resolve an incompatibility.

This chapter specifies the adopted workflow and review requirements. It does not certify a completed production agent, alter historical T4 rows, or imply that T3/T6/T6E have moved to the same route. Suitability remains scoping-grade; local feasibility must still establish conditions, design and implementation requirements.
