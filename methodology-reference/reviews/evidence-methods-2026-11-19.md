# Evidence methods editorial audit

Scope: all nine Evidence methods chapters, their cross-links, and the distinction between the pinned analytical edition and later accepted workflows. No evidence records, numerical policies, compiler outputs or scientific approvals are changed.

## Examples and chapter responsibilities

- **Source appraisal:** one hypothetical report supplies occurrence context, measured effects, costs and published expert judgement. The source record does not give every claim the same basis.
- **Discovery:** reuse in a new table scope differs from a duplicate document or a companion paper sharing data.
- **Acquisition:** a verified DOI can accompany the wrong downloaded artefact.
- **Extraction:** one results table contains measured and judgement-based claims; format and basis remain separate.
- **Review:** retaining a source profile does not approve numerical transfer; a correct effect number can still have bundled attribution.
- **Common synthesis:** nine records in four publications can represent two underlying experiments. Shared authors neither prove duplication nor establish independence.
- **T4:** observed rainfall, regional classes and compatible independent profiles lead to different proposals and holds.
- **T3/T6:** three equal-weight findings illustrate the distinction between conflicting signs and the compiler's all-non-significant branch.
- **T6E:** repeated establishment costs, annual maintenance and another income band cannot create one comparable range.

All invented quantities are labelled hypothetical. The rainfall plausibility warning already in the review contract remains explicitly attributed to that contract. Examples do not add evidence to a register.

## Duplication removed

The full magnitude-band table and comparator lookup are no longer repeated in extraction. Extraction explains what to record; effect and economic chapters own synthesis consequences. Bundle and counterfactual discounts likewise have one numerical specification in the effect chapter. Source appraisal owns the basis/format distinction; common synthesis owns the dependence audit. Short reminders remain where a chapter must be interpretable on its own.

## Contradictions and overclaims corrected

- Global suitability models are candidate profiles for agent appraisal, not automatically approved T4 defaults. Historical T4 factors no longer point to the new agent chapter as though it specified them.
- Non-adoption prompts investigation; it does not prove an identified binding constraint.
- Deterministic text retrieval and checks do not make extraction interpretation deterministic. A matching quote does not prove correct scientific interpretation.
- A DOI check and an artefact identity check are separate requirements.
- New T3/T6 per-species performance policy is distinguished from the older compiler's exclusion and from the continuing T4 species-envelope restriction.
- Current hosted review receipts and protected application are distinguished from historical local Apply, conflict reopening and CSV replay.
- A generic Keep does not establish approval of variable use, direction and calibration simultaneously. Metadata uses its dedicated fields.
- The effect class floor uses source identifiers and legacy basis labels; it does not prove independent replication or that a table contains measurements.
- The all-non-significant `no_relationship` branch is not proof of zero effect. A direction-only slight class is not a measured magnitude.
- The fixed confidence matrix is a project operationalisation, not a numerical algorithm mandated by IPCC guidance. Transfer weighting can affect agreement and therefore confidence.
- Ordinal cost classes can use one qualifying source; the legacy helper's two-source fallback wording does not change that rule. Monetary range gates remain stricter.
- Reproducing WOCAT's reported currency conversion differs from harmonising across sources. Ordinal classes remain sensitive to price years and cost scope.

## Remaining implementation and scientific limitations

These are exposed in the chapters, not silently repaired through prose:

- Source lineage and within-source share factors do not resolve unrecorded dataset overlap or sampling covariance. The existing source-contribution plan remains relevant.
- The pinned effect compiler retains mixed basis/format factors, inconsistent historical definitions of measured backing, a direction-only strength default, cross-family voting and its original non-significance convention.
- Effect magnitude bands and some policy factors remain pending ratification. Numeric policy changes need separate scientific acceptance and regenerated outputs.
- New T4 agent synthesis is an adopted workflow; this edit does not certify a production agent run.
- The current classification rollout and historical analytical edition are separate. Preserving history is not reappraising it.
- Cost synthesis does not harmonise price years or estimate a local budget; its source-count gates do not prove economic comparability.

## Verification

The documentation build checks chapter directives, source paths, internal links, numbering and captions, and agreement with the pinned data package. Worked effect arithmetic is checked against the pinned reconciliation function. Visual inspection covers the revised examples and their numbered tables. CI and publication results are recorded on the accompanying pull requests.
